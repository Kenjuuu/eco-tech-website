Our Sustainability Action Plan

Small, consistent choices can help reduce waste, conserve resources, and encourage a cleaner community. Start with the checklist below.

Carbon Footprint Checklist

Tick the actions you already practice or want to work on. This checklist is a simple personal reminder, not a scientific measurement of your carbon footprint.

Everyday actions

A Simple Weekly Action Plan

Choose manageable actions and repeat them. Sustainable habits become more effective when they are practical and consistent.

  1. At home: Check lights, fans, and appliances before leaving a room.
  2. At school: Bring reusable supplies and avoid unnecessary single-use packaging.
  3. In the community: Keep public spaces clean and follow local waste-segregation rules.
  4. Every week: Review your checklist and choose one new habit to practice.
