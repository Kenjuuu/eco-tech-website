Environmental Issues

Environmental problems affect people, wildlife, and natural resources. Understanding these issues helps us make responsible choices and support practical solutions.

Climate Change

Greenhouse gas emissions trap heat in the atmosphere. This contributes to rising temperatures, changing rainfall patterns, and more intense climate-related risks.

Deforestation

Clearing forests can destroy wildlife habitats, reduce biodiversity, and weaken nature's ability to absorb and store carbon.

Plastic Pollution

Plastic waste can remain in the environment for a long time and harm wildlife, waterways, and marine ecosystems.

How Green Technology Helps

Green technology uses knowledge and innovation to reduce environmental harm, use resources more efficiently, and support cleaner communities.

Cleaner Energy for a Better Future

Renewable energy comes from sources that are naturally replenished, such as sunlight, wind, and flowing water. These technologies can reduce dependence on fossil fuels when planned and managed responsibly.

Start With Small Actions

Sustainability is not only about large inventions. Saving electricity, reducing waste, and conserving water are useful steps that individuals and communities can take.

Reduce Waste

Choose reusable items, avoid unnecessary packaging, and sort waste properly.

Save Resources

Switch off unused lights, conserve water, and use only what you need.
